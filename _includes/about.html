About Me

An interior and spatial designer with a strong interest in adaptive reuse and the emotional connection between people and space. My design practice focuses on transforming existing structures and forgotten urban sites into spaces that evoke memory,interaction, and inclusivity. I value material honesty, sensory experience, and sustainable design approaches that respect both the past and the environment.

Core Courses: Architectural Interior Drafting, Interior Design, Architectural Design, Landscape Design
